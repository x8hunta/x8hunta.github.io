Corruption of the Feed: Triangulation Over Trust

Any single data feed, however trusted, is a single point of failure.

What happened

This is a simulated scenario I designed for practice. It follows on from the Kingston Plaza tokenization.

The tokenized plaza pays investors based on occupancy data sent to the smart contract by a data feed (called an oracle). The property manager tampers with that feed. It reports 98% occupancy when the real figure is 60%, and the contract pays out dividends based on the lie.

The technician’s view

The oracle is buggy or its connection was hacked. Switch data providers, or require several signatures on the feed.

A different provider fed by the same property manager will carry the same lie. Extra signatures don’t help if the data is false before anyone signs it.

The architect’s view

This is garbage in, garbage out. The contract trusted a single source, and nothing checked the digital claim against physical reality.

Occupied buildings use electricity and water. Empty ones don’t. That gives us a second source of truth the property manager can’t easily fake: the utility meters.

The control: Multi-Source Verification with a Circuit Breaker

  1. Physical truth anchor: read-only feeds from the electricity and water utilities (JPS and NWC in Jamaica) are compared with the reported occupancy.
  2. Automatic suspension: if the two sources disagree by more than a set threshold (15%, for example), the contract freezes dividends, the feed provider loses part of a deposit it posted as a guarantee, and an audit request goes out automatically.
  3. Human resolution: the contract only detects the problem. A professional trustee inspects the property in person and signs a sworn statement, and only that can override what the blockchain says.

How to prove it works

Test it. Feed a false occupancy number into a test environment and confirm the freeze fires, the audit request is sent, and payouts stay stopped until the trustee signs off.

Takeaway

Don’t ask whether a source is trusted. Ask what independent evidence would expose it if it lied.
