From technician to governance architect

I started out fixing things: building out offices, deploying networks, tracking fleets and inventory. This site documents the move from fixing the incident to governing the whole class of failure.

Each article comes from a challenge I worked through. It takes a real or simulated failure, shows the technician’s fix, then designs the governance control that would have stopped it, and explains how to prove that control works.
